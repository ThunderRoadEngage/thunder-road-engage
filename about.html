Goals

Keep every name in front of the public
A case that is talked about is a case that can still be solved. We build lasting, searchable case pages and share them where people in the area will see them.
Stand beside families
Families should not have to carry a cold case alone. We help them understand the process, prepare for conversations with investigators, and find support.
Give investigators clean information
Tips and public records are sorted, sourced and dated before anything is passed along, so the people working the case can act on it quickly.
Remove cost as a barrier
Every service we offer is free. No family is turned away because of what they can or cannot pay.

Methods

Every case moves through the same steps, in this order.

  1. Listen to the family

    We start with a conversation. The family tells us who their loved one is, what happened, and what they hope for. Nothing is published without their consent.

  2. Gather the public record

    News archives, court filings, NamUs entries and agency releases are collected into one timeline, with a source for every line.

  3. Coordinate with investigators

    We contact the agency holding the case, confirm what can be shared, and agree on how tips should reach them.

  4. Publish and share

    The case page goes live here, along with flyers and posts for the communities where the person lived and was last seen.

  5. Follow through

    We revisit each case on its anniversaries and whenever new information surfaces, and we stay in touch with the family for as long as they want us to.

Mission statement

Thunder Street Ministry exists to bring hidden things into the light: to restore names to the public memory, to walk with grieving families at no cost, and to serve the truth with patience, care and faith.

Rooted in Luke 8:17 and Micah 6:8
